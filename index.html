Come Watch Together.

our manifesto.

We love watching films together. In the dark. With friends. With strangers. On a big screen.

Somewhere along the way, convenience won. Streaming. Scrolling. “I’ll catch it later.” So once a month, we do things differently.

One film. No algorithm. No second screen. No half-watching.

After the credits… stay. Drink. Eat. Agree. Disagree. Declare cinema dead. Or fall back in love with it again.

Join a community of creatives, nerds, cat lovers, insomniacs, vinyl collectors, overthinkers, romantics, and people who still believe a dark theater can change your life. Welcome to filmhouse.
